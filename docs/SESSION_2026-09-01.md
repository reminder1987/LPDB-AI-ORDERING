## 4. Previously verified ordering behavior that must not be broken

The following behavior has already been tested and is considered working:

- Tenant isolation through `X-Tenant` and `TenantContext`.
- Persistent conversations and sessions.
- Customer identity using tenant/channel/external identity support.
- Product interpretation and validation.
- Ingredient modifications.
- Cheese semantics: `CON QUESO`, `CON EXTRA QUESO`, and explicit mozzarella handling.
- Valid and invalid base changes.
- **Only AREPA ↔ PATACÓN** base changes are allowed.
- Other product categories, such as PERROS, do not gain base-change capability.
- Combo flow and beverage selection.
- Invalid beverage recovery.
- Confirmation before order creation.
- Real order creation and persistence.
- Modification persistence through `OrderItemModificationDB`.
- Intentional NO-OP behavior when a requested removal targets an ingredient that is not part of the recipe.

**Important:** These business rules are not to be redesigned during WhatsApp channel work.

## 6. Agreed plan for Fase 13.1 — WhatsApp

Work on Fase 13.1 starts on **2026-09-02**.

The agreed approach is incremental, architecture-first, and based on the existing backend. Do not begin by connecting a real WhatsApp account.

### 13.1.1 Architecture of the channel

Target flow:

```text
WhatsApp
   ↓
Webhook
   ↓
Channel Adapter
   ↓
/agent/message
   ↓
conversation_service
   ↓
existing ordering services
   ↓
response
   ↓
Channel Adapter
   ↓
WhatsApp
```

The channel must not own ordering business logic.

### 13.1.2 Customer identity

Use the existing customer/channel model with:

```text
channel = whatsapp
external_id = WhatsApp external identity
customer_id = LPDB customer identity
```

Identity remains tenant-scoped.

### 13.1.3 Message reception

Build the webhook/channel boundary that:

- receives the incoming message;
- validates the incoming structure;
- identifies the customer;
- recovers the conversation/session;
- sends the message into `/agent/message` / existing conversation flow.

### 13.1.4 Response

Prepare a channel adapter that converts the agent response into the format required by WhatsApp without duplicating conversation or order rules.

### 13.1.5 Persistence

Preserve continuity of:

- customer;
- channel/external identity;
- conversation/session;
- tenant context.

### 13.1.6 Security

Prepare the webhook boundary for:

- webhook verification;
- secrets/environment variables;
- request validation;
- channel-specific security.

### 13.1.7 End-to-end testing

Before depending on external WhatsApp credentials/infrastructure, simulate the channel end to end:

```text
simulated WhatsApp message
        ↓
webhook
        ↓
channel adapter
        ↓
agent
        ↓
conversation
        ↓
order
        ↓
response
```
